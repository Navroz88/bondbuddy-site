Example: in Queensland, the clock is short

Three deadlines from the Residential Tenancies Authority (RTA) that decide most bond outcomes.

7days

to return your signed entry condition report after you move in or receive it.

14days

to dispute a bond claim after the RTA sends you a Notice of claim. Miss it and the claim is paid out.

7days

to apply to QCAT once the RTA issues a Notice of unresolved dispute.

Ten minutes per room. Proof for the whole lease.

Most bond fights come down to one question: what did it look like when you moved in? BondBuddy makes sure you can answer it.

1

Walk it room by room

Each room comes with its own checklist, from benchtops and oven in the kitchen to the shower screen in the bathroom. Tap a badge to mark it Good, Fair or Damaged.

2

Photograph the problems

Add photos and a note to any item. Every photo is stamped with the date and time it was taken, so nobody can say the mark appeared later.

3

Send the report

Export a clean PDF with every room, condition and dated photo. Email it to your agent with your entry report, and save a copy in Files for the day you move out.
